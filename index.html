Shorts Studio · 쇼츠 스튜디오

A personal desktop tool an individual creator uses to make short videos and upload them, with their approval, to their own YouTube channel.

개인 창작자가 자기 컴퓨터에서 짧은 영상을 만들고, 승인한 영상을 자기 유튜브 채널에 올리는 개인용 프로그램입니다.

Shorts Studio uses YouTube API Services to upload each video the creator approves to the creator's own YouTube channel and to show the processing and privacy status of those uploads. By using Shorts Studio you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

쇼츠 스튜디오는 승인한 영상을 자기 채널에 올리고 그 처리 상태를 보여 주기 위해 YouTube API 서비스를 사용합니다. 쇼츠 스튜디오를 쓰면 YouTube 서비스 약관에 동의하는 것입니다.

How it works

  1. The creator makes a video on their own computer: script, narration, images and rendering.
  2. Before connecting YouTube, the creator agrees to the policies below and signs in with Google.
  3. For each upload, Shorts Studio shows exactly what will be sent (channel, title, description, tags, privacy, AI disclosure) and uploads only after the creator approves it.
  4. Videos with AI-generated imagery are marked as altered or synthetic content.
  5. Disconnect at any time: the Google sign-in is revoked at once and deleted from the computer.
