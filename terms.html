Shorts Studio is a personal desktop tool that an individual creator runs on their own computer to make short videos and upload them to their own YouTube channel.

Shorts Studio uploads a video only after you review its title, description and settings and approve that upload. Uploads are private by default; making a video public is a separate step you take. Shorts Studio does not post, like, comment or perform any other action on YouTube.

You are responsible for the videos you upload and for following the YouTube Community Guidelines, including disclosing altered or synthetic (AI) content, which Shorts Studio sets on every upload that contains it.
