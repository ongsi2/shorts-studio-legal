Privacy Policy

Shorts Studio · Effective 7 October 2026

Shorts Studio is a personal desktop tool. An individual creator runs it on their own computer to make short videos and to upload them, one approved video at a time, to their own YouTube channel. It is not a hosted service and has no other users.

YouTube API Services

Shorts Studio uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the channel you connect and to show the processing and privacy status of those uploads. By using these features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is accessed

  • With your permission (Google sign-in), the identifier and name of the YouTube channel you choose, so the tool can show which channel is connected and refuse to upload to any other channel.
  • The status of videos you upload with the tool (whether YouTube has processed them and their privacy setting).

Shorts Studio does not read your other videos, comments, subscribers, analytics, e-mail or contacts.

What data is stored, and where

  • Sign-in token: stored only on your own computer (in your user profile folder). It is never sent anywhere except to Google to perform the actions you approve.
  • Upload records: for each video you upload, its YouTube video ID, title, channel name, privacy status and upload time, stored only on your own computer in the project folder.

How data is used and shared

This data is used only to upload the videos you approve and to show you their status. It is not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to anyone. Shorts Studio does not use cookies, does not track devices, has no analytics and serves no advertising, and no third party serves ads or collects information through it.

Retention and deletion

  • Stored upload status is refreshed from YouTube when it is shown and at least every 30 days, or deleted.
  • When you disconnect YouTube in Shorts Studio, the sign-in token is revoked with Google immediately and deleted from your computer.
  • You can delete the upload records at any time by deleting the files, or ask us to (see Contact); requests are completed within 7 days.

How to revoke access

Use Disconnect YouTube in Shorts Studio, or remove “Shorts Studio” from the apps with access to your account in your Google Account security settings. Access you revoke through Google is honoured, and any stored data related to it is deleted within 30 days.

Contact

Questions or privacy complaints: ongsya@gmail.com

YouTube API 서비스

쇼츠 스튜디오는 연결한 채널에 영상을 올리고 그 처리 상태와 공개 범위를 보여 주기 위해 YouTube API 서비스를 사용합니다. 이 기능을 쓰면 YouTube 서비스 약관에 동의하는 것입니다. Google의 정보 처리 방식은 Google 개인정보처리방침을 참고하세요.

접근하는 정보

  • Google 로그인으로 허락받은 범위에서, 선택한 유튜브 채널의 ID와 이름. 어느 채널에 연결됐는지 보여 주고, 다른 채널에는 올리지 않도록 확인하는 데 씁니다.
  • 이 프로그램으로 올린 영상의 처리 상태와 공개 범위.

다른 영상, 댓글, 구독자, 통계, 이메일, 연락처는 읽지 않습니다.

저장하는 정보와 위치

  • 로그인 정보(토큰): 사용자 본인 컴퓨터의 사용자 폴더에만 저장합니다. 승인한 작업을 위해 Google에 보내는 것 말고는 어디에도 보내지 않습니다.
  • 업로드 기록: 올린 영상마다 영상 ID, 제목, 채널 이름, 공개 범위, 올린 시각을 본인 컴퓨터의 프로젝트 폴더에만 저장합니다.

이용과 제공

이 정보는 승인한 영상을 올리고 그 상태를 보여 주는 데만 씁니다. 누구에게도 제공·판매·공개하지 않습니다. 쿠키, 기기 추적, 분석 도구, 광고를 쓰지 않으며 제3자가 이 프로그램을 통해 광고를 하거나 정보를 모으지 않습니다.
