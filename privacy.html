Privacy Policy

Shorts Studio · Effective 7 October 2026

Shorts Studio is a personal desktop tool. An individual creator runs it on their own computer to make short videos and to upload them, one approved video at a time, to their own YouTube channel. It is not a hosted service and has no other users. Connecting YouTube requires agreeing to this policy first.

YouTube API Services

Shorts Studio uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the channel you connect and to show the processing and privacy status of those uploads. By using these features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. How Google handles your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy at https://www.google.com/policies/privacy.

What data is accessed

Shorts Studio asks Google only for permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to view your YouTube account (youtube.readonly). It uses them for:

  • Uploading each video you approve, with the title, description, tags, category, language, privacy setting and AI disclosure you reviewed.
  • The identifier and name of the YouTube channel you connect, so the tool can show which channel is connected and refuse to upload to any other channel.
  • The status of videos you upload with the tool (whether YouTube has processed them and their privacy setting).

Shorts Studio does not read your other videos, comments, subscribers, analytics, e-mail or contacts.

What data is stored, and where

  • Sign-in token: stored only on your own computer (in your user profile folder). It is never sent anywhere except to Google to perform the actions you approve.
  • Upload records: for each video you upload, its YouTube video ID, title, channel name, privacy status and upload time, stored only on your own computer in the project folder.

How data is used and shared

This data is used only to upload the videos you approve and to show you their status. It is not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to anyone, inside or outside Shorts Studio. Shorts Studio does not use cookies, does not store or read information on your device for tracking, has no analytics and serves no advertising, and no third party serves ads or collects information through it.

Retention and deletion

  • Stored upload records are refreshed from YouTube when they are shown and at least every 30 days. The record of a video that no longer exists on YouTube is deleted.
  • When you disconnect YouTube in Shorts Studio, the sign-in token is revoked with Google immediately and deleted from your computer. If you do not connect again within 7 days, the upload records are deleted too.
  • If you revoke access in your Google Account instead, Shorts Studio notices when it next checks its connection (when it starts, and once a day while it runs). It then deletes the sign-in token, and deletes the upload records within 7 days unless you connect again.
  • You can delete the upload records at any time by deleting the files, or ask us to (see Contact); requests are completed within 7 days.

How to revoke access

Besides Disconnect YouTube in Shorts Studio, you can revoke Shorts Studio's access to your data at any time on the Google security settings page at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

YouTube API 서비스

쇼츠 스튜디오는 연결한 채널에 영상을 올리고 그 처리 상태와 공개 범위를 보여 주기 위해 YouTube API 서비스를 사용합니다. 이 기능을 쓰면 YouTube 서비스 약관에 동의하는 것입니다. Google의 정보 처리 방식은 Google 개인정보처리방침(https://www.google.com/policies/privacy)을 참고하세요.

접근하는 정보

쇼츠 스튜디오는 Google에 영상 업로드(youtube.upload)와 유튜브 계정 보기(youtube.readonly) 권한만 요청하며, 다음에만 씁니다.

  • 사용자가 확인한 제목·설명·태그·카테고리·언어·공개 범위·AI 사용 표시로, 승인한 영상을 올리는 일.
  • 연결한 유튜브 채널의 ID와 이름. 어느 채널에 연결됐는지 보여 주고, 다른 채널에는 올리지 않도록 확인하는 데 씁니다.
  • 이 프로그램으로 올린 영상의 처리 상태와 공개 범위.

다른 영상, 댓글, 구독자, 통계, 이메일, 연락처는 읽지 않습니다.

저장하는 정보와 위치

  • 로그인 정보(토큰): 사용자 본인 컴퓨터의 사용자 폴더에만 저장합니다. 승인한 작업을 위해 Google에 보내는 것 말고는 어디에도 보내지 않습니다.
  • 업로드 기록: 올린 영상마다 영상 ID, 제목, 채널 이름, 공개 범위, 올린 시각을 본인 컴퓨터의 프로젝트 폴더에만 저장합니다.
