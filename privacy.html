Privacy Policy

Shorts Studio · Effective 28 October 2026

Shorts Studio is a personal desktop tool. An individual creator runs it on their own computer to make short videos and to upload them, one approved video at a time, to their own YouTube channel. It is not a hosted service and has no other users. Connecting YouTube requires agreeing to this policy first.

YouTube API Services

Shorts Studio uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the channel you connect, to show the processing and privacy status of those uploads and how they are doing, and to put them in the channel's playlists. By using these features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. How Google handles your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy at https://www.google.com/policies/privacy.

What data is accessed

Shorts Studio asks Google only for permission to upload videos (youtube.upload), to view your YouTube account (youtube.readonly) to manage your YouTube content (youtube.force-ssl), the narrowest permission YouTube offers for adding a video to a playlist, to view YouTube Analytics reports for your channel (yt-analytics.readonly), and to create files in your Google Drive (drive.file, which lets it see only the files it created itself). It uses them for:

  • Uploading each video you approve, with the title, description, tags, category, language, privacy setting and AI disclosure you reviewed.
  • The identifier and name of the YouTube channel you connect, so the tool can show which channel is connected and refuse to upload to any other channel.
  • The status of videos you upload with the tool (whether YouTube has processed them and their privacy setting) and their public view, like and comment counts.
  • YouTube Analytics numbers, read-only: for the videos you upload with the tool, their views, engaged views, average view duration and percentage viewed, and subscribers gained; for the channel as a whole, views and subscribers gained per day. Nothing else is read from YouTube Analytics.
  • Putting each video you upload with the tool into the playlist on your channel named after its series, and creating that playlist (public) if your channel has none by that name. For this it reads your playlists' titles and which videos they hold. It never removes anything from a playlist.
  • Only when you ask for it, changing the scheduled publish time or the privacy setting (public, unlisted, private) of a video on your channel. Apart from the playlists and these two settings, it does not use the youtube.force-ssl permission to edit or delete videos, titles, descriptions, comments, captions or anything else.
  • Backing up the files of each video you make (the video, its thumbnail, images, narration and script texts) to a folder it creates in your own Google Drive. It cannot see any other file in your Drive. Upload records are not part of the backup.
  • When you ask for it, a statistics sheet: one Google Sheet it creates in your own Google Drive with the upload records of the videos uploaded with the tool, the numbers above and a calendar of their release times. Each refresh replaces its contents. It is shared with no one unless you share it yourself.

Apart from your playlists, the numbers above and the files it created in your Drive, Shorts Studio does not read your other videos, files, comments, the list of your subscribers, other analytics, e-mail or contacts.

What data is stored, and where

  • Sign-in token: stored only on your own computer (in your user profile folder). It is never sent anywhere except to Google to perform the actions you approve.
  • Upload records: for each video you upload, its YouTube video ID, title, channel name, privacy status and upload time, stored on your own computer in the project folder, and in the statistics sheet if you use it.
  • Statistics: the counts and Analytics numbers above, kept only in the statistics sheet: on your own computer (output/stats.xlsx in the project folder, and output/stats.csv if you export one) and in the Google Sheet it created in your Drive.

How data is used and shared

This data is used only to upload the videos you approve, to show you their status and how they are doing, and to put them in their playlists. It is not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to anyone, inside or outside Shorts Studio. Shorts Studio does not use cookies, does not store or read information on your device for tracking, has no analytics and serves no advertising, and no third party serves ads or collects information through it.

Retention and deletion

  • Stored upload records are refreshed from YouTube when they are shown and at least every 30 days. The record of a video that no longer exists on YouTube is deleted.
  • The statistics sheet is refreshed from YouTube each time it is updated (once a day while it is in use). It is deleted, in your Drive and on your computer, together with the upload records, and you can delete it yourself at any time.
  • When you disconnect YouTube in Shorts Studio, the sign-in token is revoked with Google immediately and deleted from your computer. If you do not connect again within 7 days, the upload records are deleted too.
  • If you revoke access in your Google Account instead, Shorts Studio notices when it next checks its connection (when it starts, and once a day while it runs). It then deletes the sign-in token, and deletes the upload records within 7 days unless you connect again.
  • You can delete the upload records at any time by deleting the files, or ask us to (see Contact); requests are completed within 7 days.

How to revoke access

Besides Disconnect YouTube in Shorts Studio, you can revoke Shorts Studio's access to your data at any time on the Google security settings page at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

YouTube API 서비스

쇼츠 스튜디오는 연결한 채널에 영상을 올리고, 그 처리 상태·공개 범위와 반응을 보여 주고, 채널 재생목록에 넣기 위해 YouTube API 서비스를 사용합니다. 이 기능을 쓰면 YouTube 서비스 약관에 동의하는 것입니다. Google의 정보 처리 방식은 Google 개인정보처리방침(https://www.google.com/policies/privacy)을 참고하세요.

접근하는 정보

쇼츠 스튜디오는 Google에 영상 업로드(youtube.upload), 유튜브 계정 보기(youtube.readonly), 유튜브 콘텐츠 관리(youtube.force-ssl, 영상을 재생목록에 넣을 수 있는 가장 좁은 권한), 유튜브 채널 분석 보고서 보기(yt-analytics.readonly), 구글 드라이브 파일 만들기(drive.file, 이 프로그램이 만든 파일만 볼 수 있음) 권한만 요청하며, 다음에만 씁니다.

  • 사용자가 확인한 제목·설명·태그·카테고리·언어·공개 범위·AI 사용 표시로, 승인한 영상을 올리는 일.
  • 연결한 유튜브 채널의 ID와 이름. 어느 채널에 연결됐는지 보여 주고, 다른 채널에는 올리지 않도록 확인하는 데 씁니다.
  • 이 프로그램으로 올린 영상의 처리 상태와 공개 범위, 그리고 공개된 조회수·좋아요 수·댓글 수.
  • YouTube 분석(Analytics) 수치, 읽기만: 이 프로그램으로 올린 영상의 조회수, 참여 조회수, 평균 시청 시간과 시청 비율, 늘어난 구독자 수, 그리고 채널 전체의 날짜별 조회수와 늘어난 구독자 수. 분석에서 그 밖의 것은 읽지 않습니다.
  • 이 프로그램으로 올린 영상을 채널에서 그 시리즈 이름의 재생목록에 넣는 일. 그 이름의 재생목록이 없으면 공개로 만듭니다. 이를 위해 재생목록의 제목과 담긴 영상을 읽습니다. 재생목록에서 빼는 일은 하지 않습니다.
  • 사용자가 요청할 때만, 채널 영상의 예약 공개 시각이나 공개 범위(공개·일부공개·비공개)를 바꾸는 일. 재생목록과 이 두 가지 말고는 youtube.force-ssl 권한으로 영상·제목·설명·댓글·자막 등을 고치거나 지우지 않습니다.
  • 만든 영상의 파일(영상, 썸네일, 그림, 녹음, 대본 글)을 본인 구글 드라이브에 이 프로그램이 만든 폴더로 백업하는 일. 드라이브의 다른 파일은 볼 수 없으며, 업로드 기록은 백업에 넣지 않습니다.
  • 사용자가 원할 때, 현황 시트: 이 프로그램으로 올린 영상의 업로드 기록, 위의 수치, 공개 시각 달력을 본인 구글 드라이브에 이 프로그램이 만든 구글 시트 하나에 담습니다. 새로 고칠 때마다 내용을 바꿔 씁니다. 사용자가 직접 공유하지 않으면 누구와도 공유되지 않습니다.

재생목록, 위의 수치, 드라이브에 자기가 만든 파일 말고는 다른 영상, 파일, 댓글, 구독자 명단, 그 밖의 분석 정보, 이메일, 연락처를 읽지 않습니다.

저장하는 정보와 위치

  • 로그인 정보(토큰): 사용자 본인 컴퓨터의 사용자 폴더에만 저장합니다. 승인한 작업을 위해 Google에 보내는 것 말고는 어디에도 보내지 않습니다.
